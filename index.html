Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is REFLO?+
REFLO is a complete customer review management, private customer recovery, appointment booking, and customer engagement SaaS designed to help local businesses grow their reputation.
2. How does the 30-day trial work?+
Every registered business receives 30 days of full access automatically with zero commitments and no credit card required.
3. Do customers need to install REFLO?+
No. Customers scan your QR code or tap your link and interact directly through their standard mobile browser without installing anything.
4. Can one QR code handle both reviews and bookings?+
Yes. One universal QR code and link powers your Customer Hub for feedback collection, appointment scheduling, and customer photo/video uploads. You can turn bookings or uploads on or off anytime in your settings.
5. How does low-rating recovery work?+
When a customer leaves 1, 2, or 3 stars, REFLO immediately alerts you so you can privately call, WhatsApp, or message the customer to resolve their concern before they post publicly.
6. What is included in the Starter Plan?+
Starter is designed for solo operators and includes up to 30 customer reviews and 20 bookings per month, contact capture, basic performance analytics, in-app notification center, and 1 customer photo upload per booking (no video).
7. What is included in the Growth Plan?+
Growth is built for active businesses and includes up to 120 reviews and 60 bookings per month, media uploads of up to 5 photos or 1 video clip, interactive calendar rules, automated follow-ups, CSV/Excel report exports, and real-time dashboard alerts.
8. What makes the PRO Plan unique?+
PRO provides complete operational freedom with UNLIMITED reviews and UNLIMITED bookings, up to 10 HD photos/videos per appointment, live Webhooks & REST API keys, custom white-labeling (removes REFLO watermark), and the exclusive background phone-wake engine that alerts you with intense 4-pulse vibration even when your phone is locked in your pocket.
9. Which plan wakes my phone in the background?+
PRO Unlimited (and active free trials) is the only plan that wakes up your phone screen and triggers priority 4-pulse hardware vibration when your app is closed. Starter and Growth users receive alerts inside their app dashboard.
10. What happens if my trial ends?+
Your business data is 100% safe and never deleted. You simply choose Starter, Growth, or Pro to continue using active features.
11. How does subscription payment work?+
Payments are processed securely via encrypted, PCI-DSS-compliant gateways. You can pay with your everyday bank card, and your bank will automatically take care of any currency conversion smoothly at checkout.
12. How does 1-click Google Review copying work?+
When a happy customer leaves 4 or 5 stars, their review text is automatically copied to their clipboard with one tap so they can paste it directly onto your Google Maps page without typing it twice. Your dashboard tracks which customers clicked through so you can follow up with those who haven't posted yet.
