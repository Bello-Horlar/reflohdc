At REFLO, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data of both our business subscribers and the customers who submit reviews and bookings through our platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide our review management, customer recovery, and appointment booking services:

  • Business Account Data: Owner name, business email, contact phone number, business category, and Google Business Profile URL (used strictly for trial verification and anti-abuse protection).
  • Customer Feedback & Bookings: Customer ratings (1–5 stars), written comments, and optional customer contact details (name, phone, email) voluntarily supplied for customer recovery.
  • Payment Information: All billing and subscription transactions are processed directly via Paystack. REFLO never stores or has access to your raw credit or debit card numbers.

2. Customer Details & Data Isolation

Customer contact information is private. We strictly enforce database-level Row Level Security (RLS) so that Business A can never access Business B's customer data or reviews. We never sell, rent, or trade customer contact details to third-party data brokers or advertisers.

3. How We Use Customer Feedback Data

Customer contact details are made available exclusively to the verified business owner who received the feedback, solely to facilitate private customer recovery (resolving low-rating issues) and confirming appointment bookings.

4. Security Standards

All data transmitted across REFLO is secured using enterprise SSL/TLS encryption. Sensitive administrative actions, subscription entitlements, and payment verifications are executed exclusively through authenticated, secure server-side environments.
